## Objective and retained foundations

- Deliver one real archival claim through intake, host-bound evidence, bounded human judgment and replay.
- Spectral flow is the primary scientific direction; the conventional-flow companion remains an interoperability/performance baseline.
- Instrument-aware panel design is a proposed outcome: biological question to candidate panels and discriminating validation, not a delivered optimizer or hardware twin.
- Maintain Verification (predefined assertions) and Discovery (candidates plus next discriminating tests).
- Configure and record the exploration/exploitation mix per run; do not prescribe a universal ratio.
- Let citizens contribute judgments, agents and compute; donated compute does not confer trusted votes.
- Offer one bounded judgment per phone screen, with relevant context available through drilldown.
- Earn task-specific capability through evaluated contributions; require neither institutional affiliation nor degrees.
- Keep Node 24, strict TypeScript, metadata-only model state, content identities and revision guards.
- Keep standalone WGSL and bounded WebGPU computation; CPU metadata intake is not a kernel fallback.
- Keep PR14's explicit `nearest-f32-compiled-v1` lossy new recipe and strict default.
- Keep PR15's delivered `preserve-xml-as-raw-v1` structural-preservation contract.
- Neither contract promises FlowJo interpretation, event-membership or topology equivalence.
- Use [HANDOFF](HANDOFF.md), [decisions](decisions.md) and [delivery](DELIVERY.md) for existing contracts.

## Integration boundary, not a second science engine

- Reuse `packages/host/src/contracts.ts`, `workspace-analysis.ts` and the existing shared RPC/MCP service.
- The browser prototype independently imports, display-transforms and gates data; it is not host review truth.
- Connect a thin evidence view to host identities rather than rewriting the numerical core.
- Public gate summaries lack geometry, exact dimensions and Boolean/dependency closure: add bounded context first.
- `preview_fcs` is a contiguous bounded event page, not representative sampling or a population-density artifact.
- Remote artifact access and per-reviewer identity are not implemented; local paths/shared bearer tokens are insufficient.
- Start local with the existing human communication channel; remote delivery requires a separately tested read-only boundary.
- Defer public reputation, billing, finance, marketplace and generalized collaboration infrastructure.

## Comparator scope

- [CytoWeave](https://robert-mcdermott.github.io/cytoweave/benchmark.html): 11 simulated MCP tasks; inspect deterministic artifact graders before adapting them.
- [Burns/Samusik](https://github.com/tjburns08/claude-cytof-gating): real-data single-dataset gating experiment; useful error cases, not equivalent synthetic scores.
- [Robin/Finch](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10652-y): actual FCS analysis with expert task rubrics; its separate 170 BixBench questions are not cytometry tasks.
- Audit licensing, task API, data, reference and scoring before reuse; retain human-only/frozen-agent baselines and make no first-benchmark claim.

Literature constraints, not claims of validated cytometry UI effectiveness:
[adaptive holdout reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629) requires an explicit mechanism/budget;
[selection and inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00554) can be circular (the paper's count-model solution is not a flow-data implementation);
[correlated model errors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07962) limit independence claims from provider diversity.
These motivate protected evaluation and counterexamples, not a new statistical framework in P1.0.

## Shared claim/evidence contract v0.1 — proposed

- Task: versioned question, claim type, track, scope, task hash and allowed dispositions.
- Inputs: study/sample IDs, content SHA-256/size, metadata hash, source URI and acquisition-manifest reference.
- Analysis: workspace revision/byte hash, selected gate, complete required ancestry/Boolean dependencies and channels.
- Semantics: source datatype, decoded precision, coordinate profile, preprocessing and display settings kept distinct.
- Evidence: immutable result references/hashes, evaluator/build identity, controls, missingness and sampling scope.
- Review: task/context/result bindings, initial answer, reason, uncertainty, attribution and supersession.
- Discovery: selection/search history, tested alternatives, failed runs, counterevidence and required next test.
- Distinguish declared, computed, independently reproduced, biologically supported, disputed and unresolved states.
- Permit insufficient evidence and no novelty established; agreement never upgrades biological support automatically.
- Review records remain separate from workspace `{author, modified}` stamps and physical-action authority.
- Treat contributor receipts as claims requiring checks; schema validity and hashes alone do not prove execution.

## P1.3 — small timed human/agent comparison, then decide expansion

Dependency: P1.2 supplies stable task artifacts and separately versioned evaluation cases.
Work: compare human-only, frozen-agent-only and human–agent workflows on matched claim strata.
Separate practice from evaluation; block donor/study relatives, counterbalance order and record prior exposure.
Test independent-first versus immediate suggestions and inspectable evidence versus rationale on bounded subsets.
Keep overlays/context follow-ups switchable; test routing/calibration against equal weighting and the strongest individual.
Retain initial judgments and shared model/recipe lineage; sparse history is uncertainty, not a negative capability label.
Primary accounting: unique valid resolutions per total human minute and expert minutes per valid resolution.
Include preparation, training, repeats, failed work, adjudication and rework; report setup and any amortization explicitly.
Report false approvals with both accepted-claim and reference-negative denominators, missed rare cases and uncertainty.
Also report full-context reversals, unresolved backlog, queue/setup/execution/review/total time and compute consumption.
Discovery accounting: nominations, supported candidates, missing evidence, failed alternatives and adjudication cost.
Predeclare task-specific error margins, resource budget, comparison schedule and stopping criteria; invent no predicted gains.
Audit randomly selected unshown/rejected and high-consensus cases before considering automatic exception filtering.
Acceptance: complete timing/denominator accounting and an honest positive, negative or inconclusive study result.
Stop expansion if context reversals, correlated errors or backlog defeat the chosen criteria; revise the task, not the score.
This pilot measures feasibility/calibration; it does not certify a rare-error bound or launch a marketplace.
